**Summary Figure 1 | Fixed spatial-operator controls in MERFISH.** A single sagittal MERFISH section containing 110,927 cells was analyzed with the same expression input, random seed, GraphSAINT sampling budget of 2,048 cells, and 3,000 optimizer updates. Only the spatial operator was changed: the trainable STEP GCN, the fixed BANKSY Gaussian neighborhood-mean kernel ($m=0$), the centered first-order angular-moment magnitude ($m=1$), or the fixed BANKSY self/$m=0$/$m=1$ concatenation. Spatial domains were evaluated against 14 major brain regions and 208 CCF regions using adjusted Rand index (ARI) and normalized mutual information (NMI).

**Summary Figure 2 | Spatial assignments under fixed spatial-operator controls.** Major-region annotations and inferred spatial domains are shown for the same sagittal MERFISH section and the four operator settings in Summary Figure 1. Predicted domain identifiers were matched to major-region labels by maximum-overlap assignment solely to provide a common display palette; ARI and NMI are invariant to this relabeling. Gray in the annotation panel indicates cells without a major-region label.

**Summary Figure 3 | Graph-definition controls across spatial-domain methods in MERFISH.** STEP, GraphST, STAGATE, and BANKSY were evaluated on the same sagittal MERFISH section. Spatial graphs were constructed from coordinates. The major-region and CCF-region diagnostic graphs retained spatial edges only between cells sharing the corresponding reference annotation. GraphST and STAGATE use their sampled paths, BANKSY uses its native full-data path, and STEP is included as the sampled spatial-graph reference. The annotation-guided graphs serve as diagnostic controls, while spatial graphs provide the unsupervised analysis setting. Panels report ARI and NMI for major-region and CCF-region recovery.

**Summary Figure 4 | Graph-training and graph-definition controls across 12 DLPFC sections.** GraphST and STAGATE were evaluated under whole-graph, full-neighbor mini-batch, and sampled training. Full-neighbor mini-batches contain the complete one-hop neighborhood of each target batch. The graph used during training and inference was either the spatial graph (S) or an annotation-guided diagnostic graph (A), yielding S-to-S, A-to-S, S-to-A, and A-to-A settings. Faint points denote individual DLPFC sections and connected symbols denote the mean across 12 sections. Panels report ARI and NMI.
