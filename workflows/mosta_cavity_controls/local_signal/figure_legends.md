# Figure legends

**Local-signal Cavity control.** Semi-synthetic embeddings were generated independently at the observed E16.5_E2S7 positions from annotation-specific distributions. Only the within-Cavity embedding standard deviation was varied. Curves show domain recovery from the no-graph embeddings or one, two, four, or eight successive applications of the same local Gaussian kernel. Points show means over independent simulations. No long-range edges or label-guided propagation were used.

**Local-signal Cavity spatial recovery.** Ground-truth Cavity positions and the Cavity-mapped clusters obtained from the no-graph embedding and after two local Gaussian smoothing steps at within-Cavity embedding SD = 0.5, followed by two, four, and eight smoothing steps at SD = 4. Orange indicates Cavity; gray indicates all other tissue positions. The combined summary places the metric curves above these spatial maps.
